Defect-Detacion

AI-based defect detection that uses computer vision to automatically identify and classify product defects on manufacturing lines — reducing manual inspection time and catching defects the human eye misses.

📷

Computer vision

Automatically detects defects from product images.

Classification

Categorizes defects by type and severity.

🏭

Manufacturing-ready

Designed for production-line quality control.

⚡

Real-time

Built for inline inspection workflows.
